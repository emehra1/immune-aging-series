The premise

The cleanest test
just failed.

§ 03
The spine

Five questions,
asked three times.

They are the application questions, the printed table prompts, and the outline of the written playbook the series produces. If you cannot answer one of them in two sentences, this is probably not your dinner.

Question I · Inflammaging II · Rejuvenation III · Tools
01Modality Cytokine blockade, inflammasome, or clearance? What can you actually give an 80-year-old, repeatedly? What must a tool measure for that call to rest on evidence?
02Stratification ZEUS enriched on hsCRP and still failed. Wrong marker, or wrong node? Who has enough reserve left to rebuild? Which stratifiers are registrational today?
03Endpoints MACE takes 6,000 patients. What would a regulator take instead? TRECs, repertoire, vaccine response — endpoints or measurements? Where is the line between surrogate and signal?
04Biomarkers What survives ZEUS as decision-grade? Which markers track the intervention, not the disease? What would it take to qualify a clock?
05Trial design Is monotherapy in aging finished? Which first indication de-risks a platform? What is the minimum trial a partner would underwrite?
